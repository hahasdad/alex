CHAPTER II · 第二章

How Can I Help?

I can help you turn a first idea into a film, or explore the medieval world through the details that interest you.

我可以陪你把最初的想法变成短片，也可以和你一起从感兴趣的细节出发，探索中世纪世界。

I

MAKE A FILM WITH ME · 和我一起拍短片

Start Your Short Film

For anyone who needs to make a short film but has no experience or does not know where to begin.

适合需要拍摄短片，但没有经验或不知道从何开始的人。

View activity details 查看活动详情

What we can do · 我们可以做什么

I can help plan your project and work alongside you as a director, storyboard artist, script supervisor, or another role your team needs.

我可以帮你规划项目，并根据团队需要担任导演、分镜师、场记或其他协作角色。

My experience · 我的经历

I founded a film club and helped lead a short film as director, producer, editor, and casting lead.

我创办过电影社，并在短片制作中负责导演、制片、剪辑和选角等工作。

Time · 时间

One session of 1–2 hours.

每次 1–2 小时。

How to begin · 怎样开始

Find me in person or send me an email. Tell me what you need to make, your deadline, and any idea you already have.

直接来找我或给我发邮件，告诉我你要拍什么、截止时间，以及你已经有的想法。

II

EXPLORE HISTORY WITH ME · 和我一起探索历史

Enter the Medieval World

For anyone who loves the Middle Ages or is simply curious about how people lived.

适合热爱中世纪，或对那个时代感到好奇的人。

View activity details 查看活动详情

What we can explore · 我们可以探索什么

I can help you understand the world at the time and how people lived—from recreating a recipe to studying the history of a country or a war.

我可以帮你了解当时的世界局势和人们的生活：小到还原一份菜谱，大到研究一个国家或一场战争的历史。

My experience · 我的经历

I led a team researching medieval siege warfare, building a catapult model, and presenting our findings.

我曾带领团队研究中世纪攻城战、制作投石器模型，并展示我们的研究成果。

Time · 时间

One session of 1–2 hours.

每次 1–2 小时。

How to begin · 怎样开始

Find me in person or send me an email. Bring a question, a time period, or one detail you want to investigate.

直接来找我或给我发邮件。你可以带着一个问题、一个时期，或任何想深入了解的细节开始。

Have an idea? Start with one question.

有想法了吗？从一个问题开始。
